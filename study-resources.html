My personal method

Study with intention.

I want my study sessions to be active rather than passive. Reading alone is not enough; I need to practise, question, apply and review what I learn.

01

Identify the learning gap

I begin by identifying a topic I do not fully understand and defining what I need to learn.

02

Choose a suitable resource

I select a relevant lesson, textbook chapter or official software guide instead of collecting information without a plan.

03

Practise the concept

I work through an exercise, create a spreadsheet, interpret a data example or explain the idea in my own words.

04

Record what I have learned

I summarise key ideas, note questions and keep track of topics that need further revision.

05

Review and apply

I revisit difficult concepts and look for ways to connect my learning to practical business and data problems.

§

Learning with integrity.

I recognise that online resources are tools for understanding, not substitutes for my own learning. When I use external material, I check its credibility, acknowledge sources where required, follow my institution's academic rules and express my understanding in my own words. I also verify that a resource matches the topic and instructions for the task I am completing.
