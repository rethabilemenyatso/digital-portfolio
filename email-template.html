Enquiry About Workplace Learning Opportunities

From Rethabile Menyatso <rethabilemenyatso520@gmail.com>
To [Recruitment or Human Resources Email]
Subject Enquiry About Workplace Learning Opportunities
Message body

Dear Hiring Manager,

I hope you are well.

My name is Rethabile Menyatso, and I am a first-year student pursuing Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana School of Business Sciences. I am writing to enquire whether your organisation offers workplace learning opportunities, job shadowing or entry-level exposure suitable for a student who is developing skills in data and business analysis.

Through my studies, I am building a foundation in computer technology, statistical foundations for data analysis and business finance. I am also developing my skills in Microsoft Excel, Microsoft Word and Microsoft Access. I would value the opportunity to learn more about how these tools and concepts are applied in a professional environment.

I am motivated to learn, open to constructive feedback and interested in gaining a clearer understanding of workplace expectations. If an appropriate opportunity is available, I would appreciate any guidance on the application process or the relevant person I should contact.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,

Rethabile Menyatso Business Intelligence & Data Analytics Student Gaborone, Botswana +267 71 226 236 rethabilemenyatso520@gmail.com

The communication standard

Three habits I want to practise.

These principles can help me communicate effectively with lecturers, employers, colleagues and other professional contacts.

01

Lead with purpose

I should use a specific subject line and explain the reason for writing early in the message.

02

Respect the reader

I should use polite language, organise information into readable paragraphs and avoid unnecessary details.

03

Close professionally

I should thank the recipient, explain the next step I am requesting and include accurate contact details.
