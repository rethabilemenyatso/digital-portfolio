RE: APPLICATION FOR AN ENTRY-LEVEL OPPORTUNITY OR WORKPLACE EXPERIENCE

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in an entry-level opportunity or workplace experience placement within your organisation. I am a first-year student pursuing Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana School of Business Sciences, and I am eager to connect my academic learning with practical workplace experience.

My studies have introduced me to areas such as computer technology, statistical foundations for data analysis, communication and study skills, and the fundamentals of business finance. These subjects are helping me develop an understanding of how digital tools, business knowledge and analytical thinking can work together to support informed decisions.

I consider myself a collaborative, adaptable and motivated learner. My developing skills include Microsoft Excel, Microsoft Word and Microsoft Access, alongside problem solving, attention to detail and creative thinking. I also enjoy research and puzzles, which encourage me to approach questions with curiosity and patience.

Although I am at the beginning of my professional journey, I am willing to learn, accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for assigned tasks. I would value the opportunity to observe professional practices, contribute where appropriate and strengthen my understanding of how organisations use information to improve their work.

My long-term ambition is to become a successful data analyst and entrepreneur. I believe that practical exposure would help me build the discipline, technical confidence and professional awareness needed to work towards these goals. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my interests, attitude and developing abilities could contribute to your organisation.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,

Rethabile Menyatso
